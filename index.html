What clipper does

clipper helps creators and teams repurpose long-form content — such as podcasts, interviews, and webinars — into short vertical clips ready for social media. Upload your source video, and clipper selects the best moments, crops them to vertical format, writes a caption, and publishes them directly to your connected TikTok and Instagram accounts.

How it works

clipper connects to your TikTok and Instagram accounts through their official developer APIs. You log in once with your social account to authorize clipper, review each generated clip before it is published, and approve the ones you want to post.

Connect your account

Sign in with TikTok or Instagram to link your profile. clipper only receives the permissions you explicitly approve.

Upload your content

Bring a long-form video or a content brief. clipper generates short vertical clips and captions automatically.

Review and publish

Every clip goes to a review queue first. Nothing is published until you approve it — you stay in full control.
